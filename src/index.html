Written pattern

    Materials:
  • DK weight Yarn
  • 3.5mm Hook
  • 6mm Safety Eyes
  • Polyfil Stuffing

Flower Face in yellow

  1. Into MR, sc x 6 (6)Crochet hook 4mm
  2. *inc* x 6 (12)
  3. *sc, inc* x 6 (18)
  4. *2sc, inc* x 6 (24) cut yarn and end with an invisible finish (see video - 7 minutes in)
  5. Add safety eyes and embroider face details

Change to Petal Colour

  1. With petal colour, join yarn to any stitch of yellow piece AND green at the same time, *sc* in each stitch around, ss to first sc to join (24) LIGHTLY STUFF AS YOU GO
  2. *[ch3, 6tr, ch3, ss] all into the same stitch (petal made) Skip a stitch, ss into the next.* Repeat from * to * for a total of 12 petals. Cut yarn and weave in ends! Or, use ends to attach keyring :-)
